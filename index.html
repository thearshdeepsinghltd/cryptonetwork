Deploy Contract

Deploy a compiled smart contract to BNB Smart Chain

Paste the contract creation bytecode produced by your Solidity compiler. If your contract has constructor arguments, they must be ABI-encoded and appended to the bytecode before deployment.

Your connected wallet will display the deployment transaction and ask you to confirm it. Review the network and gas fee carefully. Never paste your seed phrase or private key here.
